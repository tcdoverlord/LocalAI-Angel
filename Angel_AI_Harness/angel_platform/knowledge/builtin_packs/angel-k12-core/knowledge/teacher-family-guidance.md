# Teacher and Family Guidance for K–12 Support

## Start with the actual goal
Before preparing learning support, gather the learner's grade band or course, the task directions, rubric or success criteria, relevant source material, time constraints, and preferred type of help. Only ask for information that is useful; avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive details.

## Use teacher-approved materials
A school may follow state standards, district pacing guides, a specific textbook, an individual education plan, language-development objectives, or a locally designed curriculum. This starter pack does not include every jurisdiction's standards. Ask for the relevant official document rather than claiming that a general note is a required standard.

## Feedback that helps learning
Good feedback is timely, specific, and actionable. State what is working, identify one or two high-impact next steps, and show an example when appropriate. Separate feedback on the work from judgments about the learner. When the learner revises, ask them to explain how the change improves clarity, reasoning, accuracy, or evidence.

## Assessment and progress
Assessment can be formative (used to guide learning) or summative (used to evaluate learning at a point in time). Match practice to the goal being assessed. A single result is not a complete picture of a learner's ability. Use multiple observations and consider context, access, language, and accommodations.

## Privacy and child safety
Avoid recording unnecessary identifiable student information. Do not request student passwords, home addresses, full birth dates, private educational records, or sensitive family details. Use school-approved tools and data-handling rules. If a conversation suggests immediate danger, abuse, self-harm, or another safeguarding concern, do not treat the AI as a replacement for responsible adults, emergency services, or qualified professionals; follow appropriate local safeguarding steps.

## Boundaries of this assistant
Angel can explain concepts, help structure study, retrieve the bundled knowledge files, and provide general examples. It is not a teacher of record, licensed clinician, official standards repository, or substitute for the student's school support team. It should state uncertainty and request the source document when exact course requirements matter.
