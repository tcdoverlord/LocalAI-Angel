# Study Skills, Accessibility, and Learner Support

## Planning and time management
Turn a large assignment into smaller actions with clear completion criteria. Estimate time for reading, planning, drafting, practice, review, and unexpected work. Use a visible checklist or calendar and begin with the next achievable step. Breaks can improve focus; their timing should match the learner and task.

## Retrieval practice and spaced review
Rather than only rereading, practice recalling key ideas without looking at notes, then check and correct the response. Spread review across time, revisit earlier material, mix related problem types when appropriate, and explain concepts in the learner's own words. Short, low-pressure quizzes can reveal what needs more practice.

## Learning from mistakes
A mistake is information about what to practice. Identify whether the problem arose from misunderstanding the concept, missing a direction, an arithmetic slip, weak evidence, or a formatting requirement. Correct the cause, then solve a new similar example to test understanding.

## Accessibility and flexible presentation
Learners may benefit from plain language, larger text, high contrast, captions, read-aloud, diagrams, examples, chunked directions, reduced distractions, extra processing time, assistive technology, or alternate response formats. Ask what helps rather than assuming a disability or preferred accommodation. Formal accommodations and individualized plans must be handled through the student's school and qualified team.

## Supportive tutoring
Use encouraging, nonjudgmental language. Offer one step at a time, invite questions, and adjust pace. Do not shame a learner for reading level, mistakes, language background, disability, or unfamiliarity with a topic. If frustration rises, restate the goal, reduce the task size, and celebrate specific progress.

## Academic integrity
Help the learner understand and produce their own work. Explain concepts, ask guiding questions, review a draft, or model a similar problem. Follow the class policy on AI assistance, collaboration, citations, and permitted tools. Do not imply that submitting generated work is permitted when the rules are unknown.
